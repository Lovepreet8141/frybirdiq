# Counter guide — one page

Print this or keep it open at the till. Full detail: `docs/SHOP-RUNBOOK.md`.

**Sign in** with your own login. **People → Shifts → Clock in.**

**Opening the till** (finance access only, usually the owner/manager): count
the float, **Finance → Payments → Till**, type the float, **Open the till**.
If it already says open, don't open a second one.

**A customer at the counter**
1. **Operations → POS.** Pick **Dine-in** or **Takeaway** first.
2. Tap items, choose options, check the total with the customer.
3. **Charge ₹…** → type cash received → **Confirm payment**. Change shown
   on screen; the receipt prints on its own.
4. Screen says "Couldn't reach the server"? Press **Confirm** again — safe,
   it can't charge twice.

**Website orders** — keep **Operations → Orders** open on the counter
tablet, sound enabled. Each card's button is the next step: **Accept →
Start cooking → Mark ready → Send out** (delivery) or **Complete**
(collection). An order **waiting for online payment** cannot be accepted —
wait, don't cook it. Delivery cash is taken by the rider at the door, never
by you — don't tap **Take** on a delivery order.

**Kitchen glance** — **Operations → Kitchen**: tabs across the top are
stations (**Fry**, **Assembly**, **Drinks**, **Pack**), then **Expo** marks
an order **Ready** once every item is done. A card that's turned red has
been waiting too long — look at those first.

**Closing orders off for a while** — POS screen, top-right banner **Shop is
OPEN for orders**, pick a reason and how long. Tell the owner when and why.

**Riders** — **Operations → Deliveries**. A rider takes their own; you don't
assign unless you have that access. Don't touch a delivery's cash yourself.

**End of shift, in order**
1. Riders hand over their cash first — **Finance → Payments → Till → Door
   cash riders are carrying** — count it in front of them, type it,
   **Receive it**. A rider can't receive their own cash.
2. Count the whole drawer with nobody looking at the screen.
3. Type the count into **Cash counted in the drawer (₹)**, **Close the
   till**. It only shows what was expected after you close — that's on
   purpose, so the count stays honest.
4. Small difference: normal. Big difference: don't recount to "fix" it —
   write down what happened and tell the owner.
5. **People → Shifts → Clock out.**

**Internet down?** Hotspot the tablet to a phone first. If that doesn't fix
it: take orders on paper (items, name, phone, time), cash in a marked
envelope, cook from paper. When back online: check **Orders** for anything
that arrived from the website, then enter each paper order into **POS**
once, ticking it off as you go.

**Never:** refund or cancel an order yourself, type a price the system
didn't work out, use someone else's login, or guess when unsure — ask the
owner.
