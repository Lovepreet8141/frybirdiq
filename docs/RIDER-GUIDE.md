# Rider guide — one page

Print this or keep it open on your phone. Full detail: `docs/SHOP-RUNBOOK.md` §7.

**Sign in.** frybirdiq.tech/sign-in, your own login only.

**Install the app once.** Open **Deliveries**, tap the banner:
- Android: tap **Install**.
- iPhone: Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
From then on, open FRYBIRD from your home screen, not the browser.

**Getting a delivery**
- **Deliveries → Available** lists ready orders nobody has taken. Tap
  **Take it** — first tap wins. Too slow? Pick another.
- You can hold **4** at once and take **6** an hour (set under Restaurant →
  Riders, can change). At the limit, finish or **Release** one first.
- **Yours** shows the address, phone and what to collect — for that
  delivery only. Never photograph or copy them.

**Leaving with an order**
- Changed your mind while it's still at the shop (**Ready**)? Tap
  **Release**.
- Once you've left, you can't release — use **Couldn't deliver** instead if
  it falls through.
- Your phone will ask for **location permission** — tap **Allow** (or
  **Allow while using the app**). The customer sees you moving on a map,
  position only, never your name or number. If you say no, delivery still
  works, the customer just doesn't see the dot.
- **Keep the app open and the screen unlocked** while you ride — that's what
  sends your position. It stops the moment you mark the order Delivered or
  Couldn't deliver.

**At the door**
- Already paid online: tap **Delivered**.
- Cash on delivery: take the exact amount, then **Delivered · took ₹…**.
  Keep that cash separate from your own money.
- Customer not there, wrong address, refused: **Couldn't deliver**, write
  the reason. You can't mark it failed once you've taken money.

**End of your shift**
- Hand over the cash you're carrying to whoever is doing the till —
  **you cannot receive your own cash back**, someone else counts it with you
  watching.
- **People → Shifts → Clock out.**

**Never:** share a customer's name, phone or address with anyone but that
customer. Never guess a delivery code — FRYBIRD doesn't use one. Not sure
about anything? Ask the owner, don't guess.
